## Formula the brain must print, then match

Shares are not regressed. Efficiency is.

```
teamPlays = pregame pace, regressed 30 percent to league pace
targets = teamPlays * passRate(spread) * targetShare
carries = teamPlays * rushRate(spread) * carryShare
rzCarries = carries * rzShare
receptions = targets * catchRate
rushFirstDowns = carries * rushFirstDownRate
recFirstDowns = receptions * recFirstDownRate
passFirstDowns = completions * passFirstDownRate
```

`targetShare` and `carryShare` are his pregame shares. A missing share is null, not the position mean. Efficiency only, halfway to the position mean: yards per carry, yards per target, catch rate, completion rate, sack rate, touchdowns per touch, first-down rates. Then `scoreOffense` / `scoreDst` on those counts. A count the scorer pays and the projector leaves at 0 is a bug.

Print this for Judkins and the other eight before any other model edit: team plays, pass rate, rush rate, target share, carry share, targets, carries, red-zone carries, goal-line carries, receptions, first downs, efficiency rates, Bush total. Hand-score Judkins 31.7 from the box. If the hand score disagrees, the grade file is wrong and the model is not on trial.

Carry gap = (actual team rush plays − projected) × projected share, plus actual team rush plays × (actual share − projected share). The term over half the gap is script or share. If projected carries are within 15 percent of actual and points are still low, it is not volume. Subtract 6 × (actual TDs − projected TDs) from actual points. If the remainder is within 4 of the projection, the rest is touchdown variance.
